# Novas regiões do céu — 0.10.65

Três ilustrações originais criadas por geração de imagem integrada. Conversão para WebP preservando os pixels e a composição. As criaturas, personagens, olhos e efeitos animados permanecem separados das pinturas.

## sanctuary.webp

Prompt: “Production painted background plate for the spectral cloud region of a 2D gothic zombie game, wide landscape 3:2. An immense mist-filled mountain sanctuary high above a moonlit cemetery: ruined ancient stone arches and monolithic shrines emerging from violet clouds, distant needle-like dark fir forests on impossibly high cliffs, weathered terraces with tiny dim cyan spirit lanterns, a deep ravine of layered lavender fog. Rich hand-painted dark-fantasy storybook game scenery. Quiet spectral teal light, midnight indigo and dusty violet stone, restrained muted peach edges. The left-center stays dark, uncluttered and low contrast for gameplay. On the right leave a broad dark pocket above a low bank of fog for a separately animated colossal ghost face. Tiny scale references, several receding planes. No face, creature, figure, large moon, playable platform, character, HUD, text, label, border or watermark. Opaque art with dark corners.”

Central-left open dark sky for gameplay, right-side perches and cloud paths for separately animated dragon and witch. No dragon, witch, person, face, character, large moon, HUD, text, label, border, logo, watermark or flat playable platform. Opaque art with subdued corners.”

## graveyard.webp

Prompt: “Production painted background plate for the sinister orbital atmosphere of a 2D gothic fantasy game, wide landscape 3:2. An immense orbital graveyard inside dark burgundy and indigo nebula clouds: distant shattered iron cathedral docks, ancient shipyard ribs and arches, chains of charcoal asteroid fragments, sparse tiny amber navigation beacons and copper-red glints. Receding layers, thin dusty carmine plasma and shadowed crevasses, upper space almost black plum. Leave left-center dark and uncluttered for gameplay. On the right, a shadowed nebula hollow framed by tiny debris for a separately animated immense leviathan or watching eyes. Empty derelict docking cradle in the distance. Atmospheric occlusion, great scale and cinematic depth, painterly textured dark-fantasy storybook style. No actual ships, creatures, eyes, faces, people, planets, large moons, HUD, text, labels, borders, logos or watermark.”

As pinturas são regiões finitas do mundo, com margens transparentes aplicadas no código. A camada orbital reutiliza a mesma textura em duas posições, com diferentes tonalidades e profundidades horizontais. Luzes, névoa e destroços usam recursos já existentes e não participam das colisões.
