# Abismo cósmico e véu de poeira — 0.10.64

Artes originais produzidas por geração de imagem integrada. Foram convertidas para WebP; o véu mantém o alfa original. As imagens não contêm planetas, criaturas ou interface. Esses elementos continuam separados e animados no Godot.

## abyss.webp

Prompt: “A richly detailed painted background plate for a 2D gothic fantasy game in deep space. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. A vast haunted cosmic abyss, layered midnight indigo and black-plum nebula canyons, intricate curled violet dust filaments, distant icy cyan starlight catching cloud edges, very distant clusters of tiny ruined cathedral-like rock spires and shattered asteroid belts, sparse far star clusters and fine cosmic particulate. Dramatic sense of immense scale and deep atmospheric perspective. The left-center is dark, low contrast and open for bright gameplay characters. The right side has a sweeping dim cyan-violet nebula arc framing an empty region where a separate enormous creature and planet will be placed. Painterly textured storybook dark fantasy art. Mostly dark blue-black, desaturated amethyst, dim teal and muted copper lights. No large planets, moons, creatures, faces, characters, UI, text, labels, border or watermark. Opaque art filling the canvas, subtle dark corners.”

## dust.webp

Prompt: “One production 2D VFX foreground dust-cloud cutout for a gothic cosmic game, wide horizontal 3:1 image, real transparent RGBA background. A sweeping broken crescent ribbon of very dark violet nebula smoke, layered billows of amethyst cosmic dust, fine cyan-lit filaments curling around the top, scattered tiny basalt fragments and dim electric teal specks inside the cloud. The dense shadowy middle of the lower cloud should hide the bottom edge of a huge creature bust when placed in front of it; outside edges dissolve irregularly to full transparency. Misty wisps rise around an open gap in the upper center, as if a colossal head emerges above the cloud bank. Rich painted dark-fantasy storybook game style, soft volumetric edges, atmospheric distance and scale. No sky, starscape backdrop, planet, creature, face, text, border, grid, checkerboard, watermark or rectangular background.”

O cenário usa regiões fixas no mundo e transparência nas margens. O véu cruza os ombros do devorador. Fragmentos, pequenos veículos distantes, luz ambiente e ondas de impacto são compostos no código com recursos já existentes.
